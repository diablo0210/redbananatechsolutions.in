# Handloom Haat photography manifest

Asset lane, RB v3 "THE OBJECT". Source: `/home/vivek/Desktop/Data/03 Photos/HH Website Photos/`
(40 Canon EOS 700D frames + 4 WhatsApp compressions; only the Canon originals were used here).
Provenance cleared by `/home/vivek/Cultre/fleet/redesign-2026/RB-IMAGERY-HUNT.md` §A: Vivek's own
shoot, own camera, no client watermark, no third-party credit — "clearly ours to publish." §B (DCH
archival catalogue scans) was read and none of that material appears anywhere in this selection.

All outputs stripped of EXIF/XMP/ICC metadata (`-strip`): the originals carry the camera's serial
number and full EXIF block, which has no place in published assets and was removed for size and
privacy reasons, not requested in the brief but the honest thing to do once it was noticed inflating
every low-res export by 20-30x.

**Total size, `assets/hh/`: 1,946,159 bytes (1.86 MB)**, against a 6 MB budget.

## One exclusion worth recording

`IMG_0343.JPG` — a sharp, well-composed shot, initially shortlisted — was **dropped**, not because
it fails the brief's stated filters (no logo, no archival scan), but because it is a close posed
portrait of a named, identifiable living designer (credited by name on the exhibit wall panel behind
him) standing for the camera. A museum crediting him on its own wall is not the same as his consent
to appear in a private company's marketing website. No other frame in the 44 raises this issue — the
rest are unnamed visitors in general crowd/candid shots, which is the normal standard for venue and
event documentation. Replaced with `IMG_0179` (an alternate angle of the ribbon sculpture) to keep
the ring at 24.

## Notes for the chapters/hero lanes

- `archive-materials` (IMG_0417) is a materials-study table (raw yarn, fibre, museum tags), not a
  vitrine of finished museum pieces — call it what it is if used against copy that implies
  finished-object display; it is the closest genuine match on disk to an "archive objects" photograph
  for the case-study slot, but it is not literally the "600-object archive" claim illustrated 1:1.
- `vishwakarma-signage` (IMG_0320) includes a small "CURATION & DESIGN — cultre" credit mark on the
  panel itself — that is our own studio's credit, not a third-party mark, fine to publish.
- No DCH archival catalogue scan, no third-party logo, and (after the IMG_0343 exclusion above) no
  close identifiable portrait of a named individual appears anywhere in this selection.
