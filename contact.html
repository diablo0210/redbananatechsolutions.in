A useful first email tells us:

  • The venue and the room: size, light, power, and whether the floor has internet (it's fine if it doesn't; we prefer it)
  • The opening date you are working back from
  • The state of the content (collection photographed? films cut? text written?): honest is more useful than tidy
  • Who the visitors are, and the languages they read
  • A budget band, even a rough one
