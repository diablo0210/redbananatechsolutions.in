Minimal data.

This notice explains what this website records and what happens when you email us.

Website analytics

Our analytics code is designed to record only page paths, the referring domain, a coarse device category, and interactions with links or buttons. It does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, full referrer URLs, IP addresses, or persistent advertising identifiers. It uses session storage only to avoid counting the same browser tab as a new session on every page.

The analytics collector uses a secure endpoint. These measurements help us understand which pages are useful and whether the site works across device sizes.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your enquiry and manage any resulting working relationship. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

Questions

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you sent us, email redbananatechsolutions@gmail.com.
